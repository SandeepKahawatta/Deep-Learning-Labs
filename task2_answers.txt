1. What happens when the number of hidden nodes increases?

The accuracy of the model generally increases as add more hidden nodes. A model with just 1 or 2 hidden nodes will perform poorly because it cannot capture the complex, non-linear boundary of the "flower" dataset. As the number of nodes increases to 4, 5, and 20, the accuracy improves significantly, often reaching around 90%.


2. Can you explain the pattern of the accuracy when the hidden nodes increase?

The pattern shows that small hidden layers (1-3 nodes) underfit the data; they are too simple to learn the complex pattern. As the number of nodes increases, the model becomes more flexible and can fit the data better, leading to higher accuracy. However, with very large hidden layers (like 50), the model might start to overfit.  The best performance is usually found with a hidden layer size that is "just right"—complex enough to capture the pattern but not so complex that it overfits.
